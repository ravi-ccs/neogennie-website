Privacy Policy

How NeoGennie handles website, chatbot, lead, and implementation information.

This page explains the information NeoGennie may collect, how it is used, and how visitors or clients can contact us about privacy questions.

Last updated: July 24, 2026

infoPlain-language notice

Privacy notice and attorney review

This Privacy Policy is written to explain, in plain language, how Champlain Consulting Services LLC, registered in 2021 and doing business as NeoGennie, handles information submitted through this website, contact forms, chatbot interactions, and implementation discussions. It is not legal advice. Businesses should have privacy, AI, and data-processing language reviewed by a qualified attorney before relying on it for regulated or sensitive use cases.

badgeBusiness identity

Legal business identity

NeoGennie is an approved DBA of Champlain Consulting Services LLC, which was registered in 2021. References to “NeoGennie,” “we,” “us,” or “our” refer to Champlain Consulting Services LLC doing business as NeoGennie.

forms_add_onInformation we collect

Information collected from contact forms and chatbot

When you use NeoGennie contact forms or the website chatbot, we may collect your name, email address, phone number, company or business name, product interest, source page, message details, and any other information you choose to share. Chatbot lead capture may store a recent local copy in your browser and may open an email draft so your inquiry can be sent to the NeoGennie team.

business_centerLead and business details

Lead details and implementation information

If you request a consultation, proposal, or implementation, we may collect business information such as your services, target customers, sales process, website or brand assets, FAQs, appointment rules, policies, SOPs, knowledge-base content, tool access details, workflow examples, and integration requirements. Please avoid submitting sensitive personal, financial, health, legal, or regulated information unless it has been specifically requested and appropriate safeguards are agreed in writing.

monitoringAnalytics and usage data

Website analytics

We may use basic website analytics, logs, cookies, or similar technologies to understand page visits, traffic sources, device/browser information, referring pages, and general engagement. This helps improve website performance, content, and lead quality. Analytics settings may vary depending on the tools configured for the website.

task_altHow information is used

How we use information

We use information to respond to inquiries, recommend the right NeoGennie product path, schedule consultations, prepare proposals or statements of work, design and deliver AI implementation services, improve website content and chatbot answers, troubleshoot issues, maintain business records, and communicate about relevant services.

hubThird-party processors

Third-party processors and tools

NeoGennie may use third-party processors and tools for website hosting, email, forms, calendar booking, CRM, spreadsheets, automation, analytics, AI model providers, chatbot workflows, cloud storage, and payment or invoicing. Information may be processed by those providers according to their terms, security controls, and privacy practices.

smart_toyAI and chatbot data handling

AI and chatbot data handling

The website chatbot is intended for general pre-sales information and lead capture. Chat messages may be used to answer questions, route inquiries, improve approved Q&A content, and help the team follow up. Do not submit confidential, sensitive, regulated, or legally privileged information into the chatbot unless a separate written agreement authorizes that use.

lockRetention and security

Data retention and safeguards

Our data retention approach is to keep information only as long as reasonably needed for the business purpose, legal recordkeeping, security, troubleshooting, or active client work. We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational security safeguards designed to protect information, but no website, email, AI system, or internet transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

manage_accountsYour choices and rights

User rights and requests

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or a copy of certain personal information. You may also ask us to stop non-essential follow-up communications. To make a request, email info@neogennie.com.
