About NeoGennie

Agentic AI Partner for Small Business

AI is no longer out of reach for small businesses. With accessible tools and focused implementation support, even lean teams can now use AI to automate routine work, engage customers faster, and make better decisions from their business data.

NeoGennie exists to help Small Business Owners grow their business, increase efficiency, and save cost by using the practical power of AI Agents.

Most small businesses do not need more disconnected tools. They need practical AI systems that can capture opportunities, answer customer questions, support follow-up, organize knowledge, and automate repetitive work. NeoGennie helps owners start with one focused AI agent, prove value quickly, and then scale into a 24/7 AI operating system that supports growth without immediately adding more employees or increasing payroll.

Our approach is simple: identify the business outcome first, design the right AI workflow, connect the needed tools, and keep improving the system based on real operating results.

Responsible AI & Trust

Small business AI should be useful, reviewable, and owner-controlled.

NeoGennie uses approved knowledge sources, includes human review before launch, and defines when sensitive or compliance-related questions should be handed to a person. Agents support workflows; they do not replace professional legal, financial, medical, tax, insurance, compliance, safety, or other professional advice.
