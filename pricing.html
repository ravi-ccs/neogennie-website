Implementation process

Your path from Business Need to AI Implementation

NeoGennie starts with the business problem, not the technology. The goal is to give small business owners a practical first AI workflow, test it safely, and improve it with real operating feedback.

1search_insights

Discover the business bottleneck

We identify the manual work, missed leads, response delays, data gaps, or owner pain that is slowing the business down.

2account_tree

Design the AI workflow

We map the owner-approved process, handoffs, inputs, outputs, tools, and human checkpoints before building anything.

3smart_toy

Build the MVP agent/system

We create the smallest useful agent, chatbot, automation, knowledge base, lead flow, or integrated workflow that proves value quickly.

4verified_user

Review, test, and add guardrails

We test real scenarios, tune responses, document limits, add escalation paths, and keep the owner in control of customer-facing decisions.

5rocket_launch

Launch and optimize

We launch the workflow, monitor early usage, capture improvement ideas, and refine the system as the business learns what works.
