General questions

What NeoGennie is, who it is for, and how small businesses can start safely.

smart_toyWhat is NeoGennie?

NeoGennie is an AI Workforce OS for small business owners. It packages practical AI agents that help with digital presence, lead generation, customer response, internal knowledge, and custom workflow automation.

storefrontWho is NeoGennie designed for?

NeoGennie is designed for small business owners and lean teams that need growth and operating capacity but are not ready to hire a large marketing, sales, customer service, or operations team.

handshakeWhat does “AI Solutions for Small Business” mean?

It means NeoGennie focuses on AI agents that can support business outcomes, not just generic chat. The goal is to help the owner capture opportunities, respond faster, organize knowledge, and automate repeatable work.

constructionIs NeoGennie a software subscription or a consulting service?

It is a packaged AI implementation approach. Each product includes a defined business outcome, setup work, and ongoing support or optimization. Some solutions may include web pages, chatbots, automations, knowledge bases, integrations, and managed AI workflows.

psychologyDo I need technical knowledge to use NeoGennie?

No. NeoGennie is intended for business owners, not technical teams. We help translate the business need into the right AI workflow, configure the solution, and explain how your team should use it.

groupsWill AI replace my employees?

NeoGennie is positioned as workforce capacity, not a blanket employee replacement. The goal is to automate repetitive work, reduce administrative drag, and help your people spend more time on high-value customer and business activities.

rocket_launchHow quickly can a business get started?

A focused first AI agent can usually begin with discovery, content and data collection, workflow design, setup, review, and launch. The exact timeline depends on scope, integrations, decision speed, and whether the required business information is ready.

fact_checkWhat information do you need from my business?

Typical inputs include your services, target customers, frequently asked questions, sales process, appointment rules, website or brand assets, policies, SOPs, and examples of the repetitive tasks you want to improve.

hubCan NeoGennie work with my current website, calendar, forms, or workflow tools?

Yes, when the tools support practical integration. Some products can start with a simple website, chatbot, forms, email, and calendar flow. More advanced business-specific process optimization and custom workflows are usually handled through NeoGennie Starship.

lockIs my business data safe?

Data handling depends on the tools and integrations used for each implementation. NeoGennie should use only the information needed for the business purpose, avoid unnecessary sensitive data, and define what information the AI agent can and cannot answer.

checklistWhat if my business is not ready for AI yet?

That is normal. The best starting point is to click Book a Discovery Call and share the business outcome you want to improve so we can map the practical AI implementation. We will identify a focused first AI workflow so the implementation is practical instead of overwhelming.

routeHow do I choose the first NeoGennie product?

Start with the pain you feel most: credibility and lead capture points to Brand Booster, more sales conversations points to Lead Generation Rocket, missed inquiries points to Front Office Orbiter, scattered knowledge points to Back Office Mission Control, and business-specific process optimization or custom workflows point to Starship.

storefrontWhat types of small businesses can use NeoGennie AI automation?

NeoGennie is designed for established service businesses and Main Street operators, including auto repair shops, restaurants, cafés, salons, hotels/motels, retail stores, professional services, field-service teams, and owners with one or more locations. Common use cases include missed-call capture, appointment or inquiry routing, lead follow-up, staff knowledge support, owner dashboards, and field-service reporting.

query_statsCan NeoGennie help business owners see what is happening across one or more locations?

Yes. For businesses with the right data sources, NeoGennie can design AI-supported dashboards or reporting workflows that help owners review revenue, expenses, profit, customer inquiries, service concerns, sales activity, and field-service updates. The exact dashboard depends on the systems, data quality, and reporting cadence already in place.

Responsible AI & Trust

How NeoGennie keeps AI useful, focused, and owner-controlled.

helpHow does NeoGennie handle Responsible AI and Trust?

NeoGennie is designed for small businesses that want practical automation without giving AI unlimited authority. Agents are configured around approved knowledge sources such as service details, FAQs, SOPs, policies, scripts, and owner-reviewed instructions.

Human review is part of setup before launch. The business reviews sample answers, allowed topics, routing rules, and handoff steps so the AI supports the workflow the way the owner expects.

Sensitive legal, compliance, financial, medical, tax, insurance, safety, or other high-impact questions should route to human review or a qualified professional. NeoGennie AI agents support business workflows; they do not provide professional legal, financial, medical, tax, insurance, compliance, safety, or other professional advice.

Data minimization matters. NeoGennie should use only the business or customer information needed for the workflow, and avoid collecting unnecessary sensitive information. Knowledge bases should also be updated and reviewed periodically so answers reflect current offers, prices, policies, and procedures.

Business owners stay in control. You decide what the AI is allowed to answer, which sources it can use, what topics it should avoid, and when it should hand off to a human.

NeoGennie product-specific questions

Detailed answers for each featured product path.

auto_awesomeWhat is NeoGennie Brand Booster?

Brand Booster helps a small business establish a professional digital presence. It can include brand messaging, a professional landing page, lead capture forms, appointment booking, FAQ knowledge base, and AI content generation.

campaignWho should choose Brand Booster first?

Choose Brand Booster if your business needs a stronger first impression, clearer messaging, a better landing page, lead capture, appointment booking, and a trusted digital foundation before investing in larger growth or operations systems.

dynamic_formDoes Brand Booster include lead capture?

Yes. Brand Booster can include lead capture forms and appointment-booking paths so interested visitors have a clear next step.

trending_upWhat is NeoGennie Lead Generation Rocket?

Lead Generation Rocket helps create sales pipeline motion with ideal-customer discovery, lead sourcing, AI-assisted outreach, automated follow-ups, appointment booking, and sales opportunity pipeline organization.

query_statsIs Lead Generation Rocket the same as advertising?

No. It is not simply paid ads. It focuses on the repeatable sales-development workflow: finding the right prospects, supporting outreach, following up consistently, and helping the owner focus on qualified conversations.

person_searchWhat does Lead Generation Rocket need from me?

It typically needs clarity on your ideal customer, service offer, geography or segment, qualification rules, outreach tone, calendar availability, and how you want opportunities tracked or handed off.

support_agentWhat is NeoGennie Front Office Orbiter?

Front Office Orbiter is a 24/7 AI-supported web desk that captures inquiries, answers common product or service questions, books appointments, and routes next steps when the owner or staff are busy.

question_answerWhat questions can Front Office Orbiter answer?

It can answer approved common questions about services, pricing ranges, process, availability, booking steps, order or service status guidance, and next actions based on the business knowledge base you provide.

event_availableCan Front Office Orbiter book appointments?

Yes. It can support appointment flow when connected to the right calendar or booking process, including guiding visitors to available next steps and capturing the information needed before a conversation.

domainWhat is NeoGennie Back Office Mission Control?

Back Office Mission Control turns your company knowledge into an internal AI assistant. It supports staff with reusable knowledge such as FAQs, product and service details, policies, procedures, SOPs, training resources, and onboarding material.

manage_accountsWho benefits most from Back Office Mission Control?

It is best for businesses where knowledge is scattered across documents, email, people, and spreadsheets, or where the owner and senior staff repeatedly answer the same operational questions.

schoolCan Mission Control help with onboarding and training?

Yes. When supplied with accurate SOPs, policies, examples, and training material, it can help new or existing team members find consistent answers faster and reduce dependence on one key person.

rocket_launchWhat is NeoGennie Starship?

Starship is the custom implementation path for businesses ready for business-specific process optimization, custom workflows, specialized AI agents, and practical automation around how the business actually operates.

account_treeWhen should I choose Starship instead of a packaged product?

Choose Starship when your need is unique, spans multiple steps, requires custom workflow design or data handoffs, or affects a core operating process that does not fit neatly into a standard growth, front-office, or back-office package.

settings_suggestCan Starship connect multiple systems together?

Yes, when technically feasible and when the systems provide suitable access. Starship is the best path for business-specific process optimization, custom workflow design, data movement, process automation, and specialized agent design.

Common buying questions before starting a NeoGennie engagement.
