How we work

Small business AI should be useful, reviewable, and owner-controlled.

NeoGennie keeps the buying conversation grounded in business outcomes rather than hype. The goal is to build the smallest useful workflow first, then expand only when the value and operating pattern are clear.

The focus is practical implementation for Metro Detroit and Michigan business owners: start with the workflow, keep the owner in control, and use AI where it can improve response time, visibility, and repeatable operations.

NeoGennie is the small-business AI brand of Champlain Consulting Services LLC.
