Business problem it solves

Make the business workflow clearer before adding AI.

Some business bottlenecks do not fit a standard chatbot, landing page, or lead workflow. Starship starts with the actual process, handoffs, exceptions, tools, and decisions, then designs a custom AI workflow that can be piloted and improved safely.

Who this is best for

Good fit signals

  • Need business-specific process optimization and custom workflows
  • Have multi-step handoffs across people, tools, or departments
  • Want a custom prototype before a larger transformation
  • Need specialized AI agents for unique operating needs
  • Want CRM/API/system connections only where they support the workflow
